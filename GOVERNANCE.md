# Governance

## Maintainer

The project is currently maintained by `MiguelFR84`.

## Decision Making

The maintainer decides scope, releases and merge readiness. Legal-content changes should be reviewed conservatively, with priority given to traceability, jurisdiction clarity and safety disclaimers.

## Review Priorities

1. No confidential or personal data.
2. Valid examples and passing tests.
3. Clear legal source or assumption.
4. Reusable structure for developers.
5. Documentation updated when behavior changes.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Regular contributors may be invited to help maintain a specific area, such as schemas, documentation, n8n workflows or procedure modules.
